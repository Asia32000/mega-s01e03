The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The parser reads 07/04 as July 4th, and the booking moves to the mirrored date: "Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July." It shows in every channel and at every tenant. This is the problem #4471 describes and the fix in the queue is for.

hint: booking-v3's form tells day-first people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant's people do what the hint says, and the form then reads the date the tenant's way, day first. The month flips just as with the parser, so the reports read the same. But they come only through the form, only from tenants on booking-v3, and the parser fix does not touch them: the hint is wrong, not the parser.

shift: bookings move by one day, nothing is flipped

The desk read these reports as a one-day move and noted them, but filed them under parser by tenant. They are not a flipped month: "Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.", "Every booking in June is one day off. Only June.", "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.", "The invite arrived before I made the booking." Same date, a different day for people in different time zones: a time-zone or midnight-conversion bug. A day-first parser leaves every one of them as it is.

Why they were merged

  • Intake tags each report from the reporter's browser, and the tag is a guess. The table shows how often it disagrees with where the report belongs.
  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone, so nobody reads the body. Wrong day on the invite fits all three problems.
  • CauseTable places a report by its tenant and channel. A one-day move from a day-first tenant lands under parser even when the desk has noted it reads differently.

What changes

  • Each problem has its own ticket, linked in the table above, with the reports it covers.
  • The parser ticket is what #4471 was about. It closes when the parser fix ships.
  • The hint and shift tickets stay open after that: neither closes with the parser fix.
  • Read the body before you merge into a ticket marked known. A shared subject line is not a shared cause.
